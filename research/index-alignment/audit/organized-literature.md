# Organized independent literature

Twenty selected papers, grouped by research function and mapped to the package’s claim numbers. SciSpace links support browsing; primary-source links support verification. Metadata below corrects identified index discrepancies and distinguishes versions. No paper in this collection validates the complete Boolean Index Alignment implementation.

## Certificates and assurance

- **[Proof-carrying code](https://scispace.com/papers/proof-carrying-code-47tcuiaiuz)** — Necula (1997), POPL. Foundational independent proof checking; compare the trusted policy/checker and supplied witness. Claims: 2,5,14,17,20,24,32,40. [Primary record/paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/263699.263712).

- **[Assurance Cases for Proofs as Evidence](https://scispace.com/papers/assurance-cases-for-proofs-as-evidence-11zvavg03o)** — Chaki, Gurfinkel, Wallnau & Weinstock (2009). Connects proofs to system behavior through explicit assurance assumptions; conceptual background. Claims: 14,16,30,43,47. [Primary record/paper](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20150004720).

- **[Proof carrying agent actions enable verifiable licensing of language model tool use](https://scispace.com/papers/proof-carrying-agent-actions-enable-verifiable-licensing-of-krfhxn4h0e9t)** — Kadyrbek & Mansurova (2026), Scientific Reports. Close comparison for independent action checking. Small reported certificate tests/microbenchmark, not validation of this manuscript. Claims: 2,5,17,20,24,31,32,40,45. [Primary record/paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-74900-x).

- **[No Certificate, No Execution: Certified Traces as a Foundation for Trustworthy AI Agents](https://scispace.com/papers/no-certificate-no-execution-certified-traces-as-a-foundation-lj747n8uytg2)** — Liu Yanglet, Wang & Capponi (2026), preprint. Close formal/proposal comparison for whole-trace permission, generation/certification separation and composition. Claims: 14,15,17,20,24,32,33,41. [Primary record/paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24462).

## Composition and concurrency

- **[The existence of refinement mappings](https://scispace.com/papers/the-existence-of-refinement-mappings-4cae6c6ovw)** — Abadi & Lamport (1988 report; 1991 journal). Refinement background; does not prove this manuscript's R for any implementation. Claims: 14,16,40. [Primary record/paper](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/abadi-existence.pdf).

- **[Runtime Verification for LTL and TLTL](https://scispace.com/papers/runtime-verification-for-ltl-and-tltl-3qkh8x1gqu)** — Bauer, Leucker & Schallhart (2011). Three-valued runtime semantics; supports inconclusive outcomes, not uniqueness of this monitor interface. Claims: 8,9,10,36. [Primary record/paper](https://christian.schallhart.net/publications/2011--tosem--runtime-verification-for-ltl-and-tltl.pdf).

- **[Linearizability: a correctness condition for concurrent objects](https://scispace.com/papers/linearizability-a-correctness-condition-for-concurrent-1txljssh1u)** — Herlihy & Wing (1990). Primary correction source for distinguishing serializability, strict serializability and linearizability. Claims: 7,14,18,25,35. [Primary record/paper](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~wing/publications/HerlihyWing90.pdf).

## Attribution and support

- **[Measuring Attribution in Natural Language Generation Models](https://scispace.com/papers/measuring-attribution-in-natural-language-generation-models-1vngk98w)** — Rashkin et al. (2023). Attribution-to-sources evaluation; motivates support labels independent of fluent generation. Claims: 4,21,30,43. [Primary record/paper](https://aclanthology.org/2023.cl-4.2/).

- **[Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations](https://scispace.com/papers/enabling-large-language-models-to-generate-text-with-2w66s2qp)** — Gao, Yen, Yu & Chen (2023). ALCE evaluates citation quality; source availability alone does not establish complete support. Claims: 4,19,21,45. [Primary record/paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14627).

- **[Correctness is not Faithfulness in RAG Attributions](https://scispace.com/papers/correctness-is-not-faithfulness-in-rag-attributions-79sflsthbd3i)** — Wallat, Heuss, de Rijke & Anand (2024). Direct evidence for distinguishing supporting citations from causal reliance; relevant to M06. Claims: 4,5,21,22. [Primary record/paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.18004).

- **[RAGChecker: A Fine-grained Framework for Diagnosing Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://scispace.com/papers/ragchecker-a-fine-grained-framework-for-diagnosing-retrieval-2fkg5vnk46rd)** — Ru et al. (2024). Fine-grained RAG diagnosis as a comparison, not independent ground truth for this framework. Claims: 19,21,42,45. [Primary record/paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08067).

## Memory and continuity

- **[Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts](https://scispace.com/papers/lost-in-the-middle-how-language-models-use-long-contexts-30zo74j48w)** — Liu et al. (2024). Position-stratified evaluation motivation; measured effects are model/task/context dependent. Claims: 23,37,38. [Primary record/paper](https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.9/).

- **[FABLES: Evaluating faithfulness and content selection in book-length summarization](https://scispace.com/papers/fables-evaluating-faithfulness-and-content-selection-in-book-1py0rh5cdp)** — Kim et al. (2024). Human evaluation of book-summary faithfulness and selection; useful external-label discipline. Claims: 21,23,38,43. [Primary record/paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01261).

- **[MemGPT: Towards LLMs as Operating Systems](https://scispace.com/papers/memgpt-towards-llms-as-operating-systems-5dauyhw7il)** — Packer et al. (2023). Memory-management architecture motivates continuity work, without proving obligation preservation. Claims: 1,19,23. [Primary record/paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560).

## Evaluation and independence

- **[Who Validates the Validators? Aligning LLM-Assisted Evaluation of LLM Outputs with Human Preferences](https://scispace.com/papers/who-validates-the-validators-aligning-llm-assisted-u6bpa42vza)** — Shankar et al. (2024). Criteria development/drift motivates frozen evaluation manifests and explicit revision. Claims: 43,44,47. [Primary record/paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12272).

- **[Do as We Do, Not as You Think: the Conformity of Large Language Models](https://scispace.com/papers/do-as-we-do-not-as-you-think-the-conformity-of-large-12upaupa596z)** — Weng, Chen & Wang (2025). Conformity evidence; consensus is not a source of authority or independent labels. Claims: 29,43. [Primary record/paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13381).

- **[Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models](https://scispace.com/papers/towards-understanding-sycophancy-in-language-models-1g36o5nvys)** — Sharma et al. (2024 publication; revised preprint). Sycophancy motivates testing evaluator dependence rather than treating agreement as proof. Claims: 29,43. [Primary record/paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548).

- **[AgentDojo: A Dynamic Environment to Evaluate Attacks and Defenses for LLM Agents](https://scispace.com/papers/agentdojo-a-dynamic-environment-to-evaluate-attacks-and-4201ufpfov)** — Debenedetti et al. (2024). Independent adversarial task environment; measure useful task success together with security failures. Claims: 2,17,20,24,31,42,45,46. [Primary record/paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13352).

## Sparse systems and geometry

- **[Switch Transformers: Scaling to Trillion Parameter Models with Simple and Efficient Sparsity.](https://scispace.com/papers/switch-transformers-scaling-to-trillion-parameter-models-5gedbyct76)** — Fedus, Zoph & Shazeer (2021 preprint; 2022 JMLR). Sparse routing background; routing weight and active-expert count are not permission guarantees. Claims: 1,3,19. [Primary record/paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.03961).

- **[Volume estimates for unions of convex sets, and the Kakeya set conjecture in three dimensions](https://scispace.com/papers/volume-estimates-for-unions-of-convex-sets-and-the-kakeya-0xq4y9ik49wt)** — Wang & Zahl (2025). Geometric theorem background only; no transfer to AI authority, fidelity or thresholds. Claims: 6,13,37. [Primary record/paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17655).

## Reading order

Start with Herlihy–Wing and Wallat to resolve two concrete specification issues. Then read PCAA, PCE and Proof-Carrying Code to position the contribution. Use AIS/ALCE/RAGChecker for support evaluation, AgentDojo for adversarial tasks, and EvalGen/FABLES/conformity work to design independent labeling. Treat Kakeya as background for the stated analogy.
