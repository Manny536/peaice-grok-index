November 5, 2026. These annotations qualify the two historical source manuscripts included in this package. ## Coleman (2026a): Index Alignment in Large Language Models

| Location / assertion | Correction or evidence still owed |
|---|---|
| §5A.4: routing entropy approaching zero yields a dense model | Incorrect in general. A fixed top-1 route has zero entropy and activates one expert. Describe fixed routing and unused capacity; entropy and number of activated experts are different quantities. |
| §5.8: expected ledger pollution grows as Θ(tpq) | A process-specific hypothesis. Define the random variable, exposure count, update/deletion process and dependence assumptions. Bounded occupancy can saturate; multiplying proposer and policy failure probabilities requires justification. No general rate is established here. |
| §§5.5/5.7: echoing indices or matching membership establishes faithfulness | Index echoing permits membership checks; it establishes neither semantic entailment nor causal reliance. The continuation separates lineage, active evidence and external support. |
| §5A.2: capability isolation automatically lifts recursively | Treat as a required implementation invariant. A role label does not establish effective privilege separation or complete mediation at each child boundary. |
| Continuity repair / contractivity | Example improvements are not an existence proof of a globally contractive repair operator. A contraction claim needs a domain, metric, operator, hypotheses and proof; real-system convergence remains open. |
| Weighted deficit | Scalar representation is not inherently compensatory. With positive weights and nonnegative binary deficits, release at exactly zero is equivalent to conjunction. Comparisons must match the release policy. |
